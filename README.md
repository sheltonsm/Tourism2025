# Tourism2025
A nice little map showing tourism and motel ratings

Another map for my web mapping class, the two data sets may not have that much in common but they certainly work!
Data for tourism revenue was gotten from visitNC. com and all the star ratings were just off google, I used Geojson. io and put all data for each feature in manually. 
The star symbol is from FontAwesome Libraries. 

I really struggled to figure out any data to map at all, so I kind of panicked and ended up just picking two things that were interesting to me, as originally I wanted to do something with the star ratings of waffle houses in NC, but all of them have an oddly normal rating? So I decided that was lame and decided to dig around and settled on this, I dunno why this map stressed me out so much as I wasn't sure for a long time. 

I think the stars look very nice and the colors on them really pop, though I didn't make a huge amount of changes, just trying to get more comfortable with things working and not working.
